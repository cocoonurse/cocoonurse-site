Baby Sleep and Crying Consultation

Frequent waking, trouble falling asleep, unexplained crying: a baby's sleep is one of the biggest sources of exhaustion and worry for new parents. I offer personalized remote consultations, by message or video call, to understand what's going on and set up routines that work — from home, at your own pace, wherever you are in Geneva or beyond.

Frequently asked questions about baby sleep

How does a remote sleep consultation work?

By message or video call, wherever you are: we review your baby's sleep habits together and build a personalized action plan.

From what age can I get a consultation about my baby's sleep?

From the very first weeks if you have questions, and especially useful between 4 and 12 months, during major sleep milestones (regressions, crib transition, night-weaning).

Does the support stop after the consultation?

No, a few days of message follow-up are included so we can adjust the routines put in place together.
