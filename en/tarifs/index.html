Pricing — Overnight Care, Newborn Care & Baby Wellness in Geneva

Every family is different: some need a single night of relief, others full support over several weeks. Here is my complete pricing — overnight care, postpartum support, baby massage, therapeutic thalasso bath and sleep consultations — no surprises, no hidden fees. Prices include travel within the canton of Geneva; a surcharge applies for the canton of Vaud (details below).

How to choose your package

A single night to catch your breath or recover after a difficult birth → Discovery Night. A gentle homecoming, with hands-on training and follow-up in the days after → Homecoming Package. A full week to build lasting sleep routines → Serenity Week. Specific needs — twins, premature babies, several weeks of support → a free custom quote, no commitment.

Frequently asked questions about pricing

Do you offer packages?

Yes, weekly packages including day and night are available with advantageous rates.

How much does a night nurse cost in Geneva?

The Discovery Night (10h, 9pm-7am, flexible timing) is 400 CHF, or 450 CHF for a longer 12h night. Discounted packages are available for multiple nights or a full week — see the Pricing section above.

Which cities around Geneva do you cover?

I travel throughout the canton of Geneva (Carouge, Lancy, Meyrin, Vernier, Onex, Thônex, Chêne-Bougeries, Cologny...) as well as the nearby canton of Vaud (Nyon, Coppet).
